I'm a Ph.D. student in Cybernetics and Economic Statistics at the Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE), and currently an Early Stage Researcher in the MSCA Industrial Doctoral Network in Digital Finance, hosted by Cardo AI in Milan.

I hold an MSc in Applied Statistics & Data Science from ASE, and I'm a researcher at the Institute of Digital Assets (IDA), a global research and education institution dedicated to digital assets and their integration into the economy and society.

My interest lies in digital and traditional finance, particularly applying quantitative methods to forecasting and risk management, particularly extreme risk. My published and ongoing work spans tail-risk forecasting, credit risk modelling, the use of LLMs and agents as research tools, and everything in between.

Jan 2026 — Current

AI for Scientific Discovery

A side project that grew out of using AI, including agents, to aid my own research — now developing into a full research project. The end goal: using solely open-source and open-weight models for research purposes.

Mar 2025 — Current

Quantile risk forecasting

Tests fine-tuned time-series foundation models and LLM-based approaches against an econometric benchmark for Value-at-Risk, across a suite of backtests (traffic light, Acerbi–Szekely). Preliminary finding: AI-based models can match or surpass the true model under small samples and heavy tails — but performance is configuration-dependent.

Oct 2024 — Current

A semi-analytic framework for DeFi long-short sizing

Models the initial sizing of a leveraged DeFi long-short position under bivariate Kou double-exponential jump-diffusion dynamics, reducing survival-path payoff moments to killed expectations solvable via a finite-dimensional Laplace-domain linear system.
